Quagga Privacy Policy

Effective October 17, 2026

In short: Quagga does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What Quagga collects

Nothing. Quagga is a synthesizer that runs entirely on your device. It does not connect to the internet and does not send any information to us or to anyone else.

Data stored on your device

Quagga saves a few settings locally on your device so that it opens the way you left it, such as your keyboard scale, key size and the patch you were using. If you load your own sound files, they are read from your device and never uploaded. This data stays on your device and is deleted when you delete the app.

Microphone, camera, location and contacts

Quagga does not access your microphone, camera, location, contacts or photos.

Plugin use in other apps

When you use Quagga as an AUv3 plugin inside another app, such as GarageBand or AUM, your patch settings are saved inside that app's project file. That data is handled by the host app under its own privacy policy.

Apple

If you choose to share crash reports or beta feedback with developers through your device settings or TestFlight, Apple collects and provides that information under Apple's Privacy Policy. We use it only to fix problems in Quagga.

Children

Quagga does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
